# Google Ads launch guardrails for Regency House Hixson

Adapted 2026-11-11 from the sister community's live account (mechanics only; no account IDs). Use this
when ownership opens the Google Ads account. Launch is an executive decision; this file is the plan.

## Five rules

1. Budgets are capped DAILY so the weekly total matches intent (daily = weekly intent / 7). Raising any
   budget or bid cap is ownership's decision, never the agent's.
2. Never auto-apply Google recommendations for budget, bids or bid strategy, broad match, Performance Max,
   or auto-generated ad/asset text. Dismiss them (dismissing raises the optimization score the same as
   applying). Turn OFF account-level auto-apply in Recommendations settings. Apply only spend-neutral
   clean-ups (narrowing match type, removing redundant or conflicting keywords, ad rotation).
3. Call conversion = calls from ads lasting 60 seconds or more. Shorter calls are wrong numbers and
   main-line lookups.
4. PRIMARY conversions, one per click: Tour booking (/visit/), Website form lead (/thank-you/), Calls from
   ads (60 s). SECONDARY (observe only): website phone click, GA4 form_submit. Do not create, or set to
   secondary, Google's auto-created "local actions" (directions, website visits, engagements, map or ad
   clicks-to-call): they inflate conversions.
5. Search partners OFF and Display expansion OFF on every Search campaign; no Performance Max; keywords as
   PHRASE or EXACT (Google's suggested keywords arrive as broad and pull foreign "aged care", real-estate
   and job searches; dismiss them).

## Campaign structure

- "Leads 1 - Assisted Living" (general) and "Leads 2 - Respite Care". No memory-care campaign: Regency
  House does not offer memory care, so memory care, secure memory care, dementia unit, alzheimer's care
  are account-wide negatives.
- Routing negatives: Leads 1 blocks respite / short term / temporary; Leads 2 blocks nothing of ours.
- Own brand (regency house, regency house hixson) kept out of non-brand campaigns.
- Out-of-market cities as BROAD negatives: memphis, nashville, knoxville, atlanta, birmingham, huntsville,
  louisville, lexington, murfreesboro, cleveland tn only if it proves unproductive (it is in market for
  some families). NEVER add chattanooga, hixson, soddy daisy, red bank, signal mountain, ooltewah,
  east ridge or dalton.
- Ad sitelinks pass ?interest= to pre-fill the contact form (contact form already supports it).

## Payer rule for ads and negatives (ownership, 2026-11-11)

Regency House is private pay AND accepts PACE participants. So, unlike the sister account:
- Do NOT add negatives for: pace, medicaid, medicare, tenncare, choices program, ecf choices, waiver,
  state funded, government, adult day (PACE runs through adult day health centers), humana, insurance.
  Those searchers may be PACE-eligible families. Review their search terms monthly instead.
- Keep as negatives (non-buyers): free, low cost, low income, income based, subsidized, section 8, hud,
  public housing, affordable, va, veteran, veterans, tricare, aid and attendance, long term care
  insurance, social security, ssi, ssdi (the community does not bill those programs).
- Ad copy never says Medicaid, TennCare, Medicare, VA or insurance "accepted"; it may say "PACE
  participants welcome".

## Lessons from the sister account (30 days)

107 ad calls, about 17 lasted 60 s or more, 13 went unanswered: phone answering beats ad tweaks. Track
tour bookings as a primary conversion. Cap daily budgets. Add negatives for competitor names, photos,
drug and medical questions.
