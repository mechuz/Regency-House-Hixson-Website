About Regency House

An assisted living community on Hamill Road in Hixson, Tennessee.

Our Community

Personal Care in a Setting That Feels Like Home

Regency House is an assisted living community in Hixson, Tennessee, with forty-eight private apartments. We have served this community for many years, and most of the families who choose us live within a short drive.

Our focus is assisted living and respite care, provided consistently and with respect for each resident’s dignity and independence. Residents enjoy private apartments, three meals a day, a full activity calendar, and a care team on site around the clock.

Our Commitments

How We Serve Residents and Families

  • Honesty about cost and fit. We discuss rates, availability and whether we are the right community for your family openly, by telephone, before you visit.
  • Dignity and independence. We assist with what has become difficult and respect each resident’s ability to do the rest. Care plans are reviewed regularly as needs change.
  • Quality at an affordable rate. One inclusive monthly rate covers the apartment, meals, care and services, and we offer seasonal discounts on select apartments throughout the year.
  • Direct, personal service. When you call, you reach our team in Hixson. Your enquiry is handled here and is never passed to a referral service.

Visit Us

Come See the Community

The best introduction to Regency House is a visit. Meet our staff, see the apartments and dining room, and join residents for lunch. Tours are available daily by appointment.
