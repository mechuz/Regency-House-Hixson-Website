About Regency House

A licensed assisted living community on Hamill Road in Hixson, Tennessee.

Who We Are

A Community, Not a Campus

Regency House is a licensed Assisted Care Living Facility in Hixson, Tennessee, with forty-eight apartments and a licence for up to fifty-four residents. We have been part of this neighborhood a long time, and most of the families who come to us live within a short drive.

What we offer is narrow on purpose: assisted living and respite care, done properly, in a building small enough that nobody becomes a room number. We are not a skilled nursing facility and we do not operate a secure memory care unit — and we will tell you so on the first call rather than let you discover it after a move.

What We Believe

How We Try to Do This

  • Straight answers, early. Cost, availability and whether we are even the right fit — on the phone, in about ten minutes, before you drive out here.
  • Independence first. Help with what has become unsafe or exhausting, and not one thing more. A care plan is reviewed as needs change, not set once and forgotten.
  • Know the limits. If someone needs more care than an assisted living licence allows us to provide, we say so and help the family plan the next step.
  • No referral middlemen. Call us and you reach the community in Hixson. We do not sell your information to a dozen facilities.

Licensing

Licensed by the State of Tennessee

Regency House is licensed by the Tennessee Department of Health, Board for Licensing Health Care Facilities, as an Assisted Care Living Facility — license #0537-00000150. State inspection records for any licensed facility in Tennessee are public, and we encourage families to look ours up.
