Our Pricing

How the Monthly Rate Is Determined

Regency House is committed to quality care at a rate families can afford. There are no care levels or tiered pricing. One monthly rate, set by the apartment you choose, covers the apartment and all of the care and services we provide.

The apartment you choose

A studio or a one-bedroom, each with its own kitchenette and private bathroom. Larger apartments are priced accordingly. Availability changes from week to week, and we will tell you exactly what is open when you call.

Everything else is included

Personal care, medication management, three meals a day, housekeeping, laundry, activities and transportation are all part of the rate. A resident who needs more help is not charged more than one who needs less.

Transparent Pricing

Your Rate, Quoted in Writing

Because the rate depends on the apartment and on current availability, we do not publish a single figure online. Call us and we will give you a clear monthly estimate in about ten minutes. Every one-time cost is set out in writing before you sign. You will know the full cost before you make the drive to Hixson.

Paying for Care

A Private-Pay Community

Regency House is a private-pay community. The monthly rate is paid directly by the resident or family from income, savings, or the proceeds of a home sale. We do not bill insurance or government programs.

Regency House is private pay and welcomes participants enrolled in PACE, the Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly; our guide to paying for senior living explains PACE and the other ways Hamilton County families plan for the cost.

Keeping it simple means no claims, no eligibility paperwork, and no waiting on an outside approval before a move-in. Every one-time cost is set out in writing before anything is signed.

Good to Know

Medicare, Medicaid and Insurance

Medicare does not pay for assisted living room and board at any community, and Regency House does not bill Medicare for care.

TennCare and Medicaid are not accepted. Regency House does not participate in the TennCare CHOICES program.

Long-term care insurance and VA benefits are not billed by Regency House. Some families receive reimbursement or a benefit directly and use it toward the monthly rate; whether a policy or benefit applies is between the family and the insurer or the VA.

Common Questions

Questions About Cost

Is there a community fee or move-in fee?

Please ask us directly when you call. We will set out every one-time cost in writing before anything is signed, so there are no surprises at move-in.

Does the monthly rate change if care needs increase?

No. Regency House does not use care levels or tiered pricing. The rate is set by the apartment, and the care and services included do not change the price as needs change.

Do you accept Medicare, TennCare or insurance?

No. Regency House is a private-pay community that also welcomes participants enrolled in PACE. We do not bill Medicare, TennCare, Medicaid, long-term care insurance or the VA. The monthly rate is paid directly by the resident or family. We do welcome participants enrolled in PACE, the Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly.

Can we tour before discussing cost?

Yes, and the reverse is equally welcome. Many families prefer to know the rate first so they can tour with confidence. Either order works for us.
