Rates & Floor Plans

Two apartment styles, one all-inclusive monthly rate, and a straight answer when you call.

How Pricing Works

Built From Two Things, and Nothing Else

1. The apartment you choose

A studio or a one-bedroom, each with its own kitchenette. Larger apartments cost more, exactly as you would expect. Availability varies, so the honest answer about what is open changes week to week.

2. The level of daily care needed

A nurse assessment establishes how much hands-on help is needed. Someone who needs a reminder and a steadying arm does not pay the same as someone who needs full assistance morning and night.

Being Straight With You

Why There Is No Price List on This Page

We could put a tempting number here, the way many communities do, and then explain the add-ons once you are sitting in the office. We would rather quote you the real monthly figure on the phone in about ten minutes — including what long-term care insurance or VA benefits may offset. If the number does not work for your family, you will know before you have driven out to Hixson.

Included

What the Monthly Rate Covers

One rate covers the apartment and the care. These are not billed separately:

  • Private apartment with kitchenette
  • Three meals daily plus snacks
  • Personal care assistance
  • Medication administration by licensed nurses
  • 24-hour care team and emergency call system
  • Weekly housekeeping and personal laundry
  • Activities programme and scheduled transportation

Paying For Care

How Families Cover the Cost

  • Private pay — from income, savings, or the sale of a home
  • Long-term care insurance — we can help you read the policy and file
  • VA benefits — Aid and Attendance can meaningfully offset the monthly cost for eligible veterans and surviving spouses

Coverage depends on your specific policy or eligibility. Call and we will go through what applies and what paperwork you will need.

A common and costly misunderstanding: Medicare does not pay for assisted living room and board, anywhere. It may cover specific medical services a resident receives. For TennCare and the CHOICES programme, call and ask what we can currently accept, as rules change.

Cost Questions

What Families Ask About Money

Is there a community fee or move-in fee?

Ask us directly when you call and we will tell you every one-time cost up front, in writing, before you sign anything. You should never discover a fee at signing — from us or from anyone else.

Does the monthly rate go up if care needs increase?

Yes — if the level of daily care changes, the rate is adjusted to match. We reassess regularly and talk to you before any change takes effect, so it is a conversation rather than a surprise on an invoice.

Do you accept Medicare or TennCare?

Medicare does not pay for assisted living room and board anywhere — that is a common and costly misunderstanding. Medicare may cover specific medical services a resident receives. For TennCare and the CHOICES programme, call us and ask what we can currently accept, as participation and eligibility rules change.

Can we tour before discussing money?

Of course — and you can also do it the other way round. Many families would rather know the number first so they are not touring somewhere they cannot afford. Either order is fine by us.
