Privacy Policy

Regency House Assisted Living (“us”, “we”, or “our”) operates www.hixsonregencyhouse.com (the “Site”). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of Personal Information we receive from users of the Site.

We use your Personal Information only for providing and improving the Site. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information Collection and Use

While using our Site, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to, your name (“Personal Information”).

Log Data

Like many site operators, we collect information that your browser sends whenever you visit our Site (“Log Data”). This Log Data may include information such as your computer’s Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, browser type, browser version, the pages of our Site that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages and other statistics.

Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data, which may include an anonymous unique identifier. Cookies are sent to your browser from a web site and stored on your computer’s hard drive.

Like many sites, we use “cookies” to collect information. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Site.

SMS Communications

If you provide your mobile number to Regency House Assisted Living through our website, by phone, or via a third-party referral service (such as A Place for Mom), you may receive SMS text messages from us regarding your inquiry. These messages may include tour scheduling, follow-up communication, and information about our community. Message frequency varies; recipients may receive up to 8 messages per inquiry.

You can opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message. To opt back in, reply START. For help, reply HELP or contact us at (423) 870-0050.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with any third party for any purpose.

Advertising and Analytics

We use Google Ads conversion tracking and related Google tags to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. These tools may set cookies and collect information such as your IP address, device identifiers, pages visited, and whether you called us or submitted a form. You can control ad personalization at adssettings.google.com and learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Service Providers

Contact form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form-processing service, and maps on our Contact page are provided by Google Maps. Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their services to us.

Health Information

Please do not submit detailed medical or health information through the website contact form. Health information about current and prospective residents is collected directly by our care team and protected in accordance with applicable federal and Tennessee law.

Children

Our Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided through the Site by contacting us at info@hixsonregencyhouse.com or (423) 870-0050. We do not sell personal information.

Security

The security of your Personal Information is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy was last updated August 21, 2026 and will remain in effect except with respect to any changes in its provisions in the future, which will be in effect immediately after being posted on this page.

We reserve the right to update or change our Privacy Policy at any time and you should check this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the Service after we post any modifications to the Privacy Policy on this page will constitute your acknowledgment of the modifications and your consent to abide and be bound by the modified Privacy Policy.

If we make any material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you either through the email address you have provided us, or by placing a prominent notice on our website.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
