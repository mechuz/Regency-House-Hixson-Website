Welcome to Regency House

Quality Care in a Community That Knows You

With forty-eight apartments, Regency House is small enough that every resident is known as an individual — by name, by preference and by routine. Our staff care for the same residents each day, and that continuity is the foundation of good care.

Assistance with bathing, dressing and daily routines is provided by trained staff, and medications are administered on schedule by our nursing team. Three meals a day, housekeeping, laundry, activities and transportation are all included.

We believe quality care should be within reach. Our rates are inclusive and straightforward, and we regularly offer seasonal discounts on select apartments. Ask about current specials when you call.

Assisted Living & Respite Care

Assisted Living

A private studio or one-bedroom apartment with a kitchenette, and a personal care plan based on an individual assessment — the assistance each resident needs, reviewed as needs change. Three meals daily, medication management, and a care team on site around the clock.

About Assisted Living

Respite & Short-Term Care

Stays of several days to several weeks — relief for a family caregiver, recovery after a hospital stay, or an opportunity to experience the community before making a longer commitment. The same apartment and the same care, with no long-term obligation.

Life at Regency House

A Typical Day

The day begins with assistance rising and dressing for residents who need it, morning medications, and breakfast in the dining room. From there, residents follow their own routines: activities, a salon appointment, time in the courtyard, a visit from family, or a quiet hour with the newspaper.

Housekeeping, laundry and medication management are handled by our staff, so that residents and families can focus on their time together.

Our Location

Convenient to Chattanooga and Hamilton County

Regency House is located on Hamill Road in Hixson, an established residential area minutes from Highway 153, Northgate, and CHI Memorial Hospital Hixson. Families find it an easy, familiar drive for regular visits.

We serve families from Hixson, Chattanooga, Red Bank, Middle Valley, Soddy-Daisy, Signal Mountain, East Ridge, Ooltewah and Harrison.
