Welcome to Regency House

An Intimate Residence, Thoughtfully Kept

There is a particular ease to a community of forty-eight apartments. Our residents are known — by name, by preference, by the small rituals of their day. The attendant who brings the morning coffee is the same one who notices when the newspaper goes unread.

Regency House is licensed by the Tennessee Department of Health as an Assisted Care Living Facility. Behind that designation is a simple promise: the assistance our residents receive — with bathing, with dressing, with the daily matters that have grown difficult — is delivered by trained hands, and medications are administered on schedule by licensed nurses, every day without exception.

Life at Regency House

The Shape of a Day

Mornings begin gently — assistance with rising and dressing for those who wish it, medications attended to, and breakfast served in the dining room. The day unfolds from there with a rhythm rather than a regimen: a salon appointment, an hour in the courtyard, a visit from family, the newspaper and a quiet corner.

Housekeeping, laundry and medications are seen to by our staff, so that visits with family may be spent simply in one another’s company.

Minutes From Highway 153 — and a World Away From It

Our address offers what Chattanooga families seek most: an established residential setting close to CHI Memorial Hixson and Northgate, familiar shopping and dining, and an unhurried drive for visiting family — without the pace of a busier corridor.

We are privileged to welcome families from Hixson, Chattanooga, Red Bank, Middle Valley, Soddy-Daisy, Signal Mountain, East Ridge, Ooltewah and Harrison.
