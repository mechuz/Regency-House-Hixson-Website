Equal Housing Opportunity Accessible Community

Regency House Assisted Living is committed to the letter and spirit of the federal Fair Housing Act and the Tennessee Human Rights Act. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability, age, or any other characteristic protected by law in the admission, treatment, or services provided to residents.

Services are provided under a resident agreement following an individual assessment. Availability, rates, and specials are subject to change. Information on this website is for general purposes only and is not medical, legal, or financial advice.

Regency House provides assisted living and respite care. We do not operate a secure memory care unit and we are not a skilled nursing facility.
