A Day in Residence

The Rhythm of the Day

Nothing here is compulsory. The rhythm matters more than the schedule — knowing that breakfast will be served, that someone will knock, that the day has a shape.

  • Morning — assistance with rising, bathing and dressing. Morning medications. Breakfast in the dining room.
  • Mid-morning — activities, gentle exercise, a salon appointment, or a quiet hour with the paper.
  • Midday — luncheon together, the most convivial hour of the day.
  • Afternoon — outings and scheduled transportation, visits from family, the courtyard, rest.
  • Evening — dinner, evening medications, and assistance retiring for those who wish it.
  • Overnight — staff on the premises, call pendants answered, and checks on residents who need them.

Do ask on your tour for this month’s activity calendar.

A Suggestion

The Truest Measure of a Residence Is Luncheon

Brochures are easily made. Instead, visit at midday and observe three things: whether residents are in conversation with one another, whether staff greet people by name, and whether the food is something you would gladly eat yourself. We will set a place for you.
