Daily Life

A Typical Day at Regency House

Residents set their own pace. Our role is to provide a dependable structure around it: meals at regular times, care delivered when it is scheduled, and staff available at every hour.

  • Morning — assistance with rising, bathing, and dressing. Morning medications. Breakfast in the dining room.
  • Mid-morning — activities, exercise, a salon appointment, or quiet time in the apartment.
  • Midday — lunch served in the dining room.
  • Afternoon — outings and scheduled transportation, family visits, the courtyard, rest.
  • Evening — dinner, evening medications, and assistance preparing for bed as needed.
  • Overnight — staff on site, call pendants answered, and checks on residents who need them.

Ask for the current month’s activity calendar when you visit, or see photos from recent celebrations and events.

Dining

Three Meals a Day, Served in Our Dining Room

Meals are prepared on site and served in the dining room, where residents eat together at round tables rather than alone in their apartments. Snacks and beverages are available through the day, and special dietary needs are accommodated. Tell us about them at admission.

Mealtime is also the social center of the day. Families are welcome to join a resident for lunch, and we ask only that you let us know ahead so a place is set.

Community Spaces

Rooms Made for Company

The front hall opens into the dining room beneath the main staircase, and sitting rooms with upholstered chairs and local prints give residents and visitors a comfortable place to talk. Every apartment is private, with its own kitchenette and bathroom, so residents can retreat whenever they choose.

Outside, the courtyard sits between the apartment wings, planted with daylilies, roses and Japanese maples, with benches along the walkway. It is a secure place to sit in the sun, and the setting for our visiting petting zoo and outdoor celebrations.

Activities & Celebrations

Something Worth Doing Every Day

Our activity calendar includes exercise, crafts, games, music and devotionals, with a celebration for every holiday: St. Patrick’s Day, Easter, Mardi Gras, luau day, New Year’s Eve and resident birthdays. Visiting animals, a flag ceremony honoring our veterans, and scheduled outings round out the month.

Participation is always a resident’s choice. Some join everything; others prefer a quiet morning and the newspaper. Both are welcome here. See photos from recent events.
