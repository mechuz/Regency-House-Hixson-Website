A Day Here

The Rhythm of the Day

Nothing here is compulsory. The rhythm matters more than the schedule — knowing breakfast happens, that someone will knock, that the day has a shape.

  • Morning — help waking, bathing and dressing. Morning medications. Breakfast in the dining room.
  • Mid-morning — activities, exercise, a salon appointment, or a quiet hour with the paper.
  • Midday — lunch together, the busiest and most social meal of the day.
  • Afternoon — outings and scheduled transportation, visits from family, the courtyard, rest.
  • Evening — dinner, evening medications, and help getting ready for bed as needed.
  • Overnight — staff on site, call pendants answered, checks for residents who need them.

Our Advice

The Best Way to Judge a Community Is Lunch

Brochures are easy to make. Instead, come at midday and look at three things: whether residents are talking to each other, whether staff greet people by name, and whether the food is something you would eat. We will set a place for you.
