Daily Life

A Typical Day at Regency House

Residents set their own pace. Our role is to provide a dependable structure around it: meals at regular times, care delivered when it is scheduled, and staff available at every hour.

  • Morning — assistance with rising, bathing, and dressing. Morning medications. Breakfast in the dining room.
  • Mid-morning — activities, exercise, a salon appointment, or quiet time in the apartment.
  • Midday — lunch served in the dining room.
  • Afternoon — outings and scheduled transportation, family visits, the courtyard, rest.
  • Evening — dinner, evening medications, and assistance preparing for bed as needed.
  • Overnight — staff on site, call pendants answered, and checks on residents who need them.

Ask for the current month’s activity calendar when you visit, or see photos from recent celebrations and events.
