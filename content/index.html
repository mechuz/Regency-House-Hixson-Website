Welcome to Regency House

Smaller, On Purpose

Large senior living campuses can run to two or three hundred apartments. Regency House has forty-eight, licensed for up to fifty-four residents. That difference is the whole point: the person who brings your mother her breakfast is the same person who notices when she is quieter than usual.

We are licensed by the Tennessee Department of Health as an Assisted Care Living Facility. In plain terms, that means our team is trained and regulated to help with the daily things that have become difficult — bathing, dressing, getting safely to the dining room — and our licensed nurses administer medications on schedule, every single day.

Life at Regency House

What an Ordinary Tuesday Looks Like

Days start with help waking and dressing for those who need it, morning medications, and breakfast in the dining room. From there the day has a shape rather than a schedule: activities, the courtyard when the weather is good, a salon appointment, visits from family, or the newspaper and a quiet corner.

Housekeeping, laundry, and medications are handled by our staff, so residents and families can spend the visit actually visiting.
