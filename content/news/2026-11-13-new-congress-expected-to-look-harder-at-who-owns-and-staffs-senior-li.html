Senior Housing News reports that the incoming Congress is likely to bring renewed attention to senior living ownership, staffing levels and resident safety, alongside fresh efforts to address affordability. The industry's trade group says its priority is keeping assisted living under state rather than federal health care rules, and it is watching whether a previously introduced bill on assisted living access returns with new sponsors. Assisted living remains licensed and inspected by each state, so families comparing communities today still rely on state records and their own visits rather than federal ratings.

Why it matters: When touring, ask any community who owns it, how it is staffed overnight, and for its most recent state inspection; those are the questions lawmakers are starting to ask too.
