A new workforce index built from the records of more than 236,000 assisted living and skilled nursing employees finds that staff turnover in assisted living has fallen to about 72 percent over the twelve months ending September 30, 2026, down from a peak above 84 percent in late 2024. The weak spot is the first three months: roughly 39 percent of new direct-care hires in assisted living leave before their ninetieth day. The index is meant to give communities a common yardstick for retention and is not comparable to the staffing measures on Medicare's Care Compare site.

Why it matters: When you tour, ask how long the caregivers you meet have worked there; stable staff is one of the best signs of consistent care.
