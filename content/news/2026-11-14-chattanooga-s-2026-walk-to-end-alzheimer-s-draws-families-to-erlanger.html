Hundreds of Chattanooga-area residents gathered at Erlanger Park on Saturday, November 7, for the city's 2026 Walk to End Alzheimer's, the Alzheimer's Association's annual fundraiser for research, care and support programs. Walkers carried the association's colored flowers that mark their connection to the disease: caregivers, people living with it, those who have lost someone, and supporters. Money raised stays partly local, funding the association's 24-hour helpline, support groups and education programs available to families across Hamilton County.

Why it matters: The Alzheimer's Association's local chapter runs free support groups and a 24-hour helpline that Chattanooga families can use year-round, not just on walk day.
