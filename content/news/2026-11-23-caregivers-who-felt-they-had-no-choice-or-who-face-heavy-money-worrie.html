A report from AARP and the National Alliance for Caregiving looks at the health and well-being of family caregivers. It finds that people who felt they had no choice about taking on the role reported more days of poor mental health, and that heavy financial strain goes hand in hand with greater emotional stress and lower self-rated health. The authors describe a gap between caregivers' needs and the support available to them.

Why it matters: If you are the family caregiver, your own health counts too; sharing the load with siblings or arranging a short break can be a practical first step.
