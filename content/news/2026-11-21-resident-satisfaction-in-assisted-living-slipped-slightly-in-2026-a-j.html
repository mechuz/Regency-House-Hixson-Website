Senior Housing News reports on the 2026 J.D. Power study of senior living residents and families. Overall satisfaction scores for assisted living dipped slightly this year, with four of the six factors measured declining from last year, while independent living scores rose.

Why it matters: Families touring communities can use the same lens the survey does: ask about day-to-day care, staff responsiveness, dining and activities, and talk with current residents and families, not only the marketing materials.
