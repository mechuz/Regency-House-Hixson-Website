The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services announced on November 17 that the standard Medicare Part B premium will be $209.40 a month in 2027, up $6.50 from $202.90 this year, and that the annual Part B deductible rises from $283 to $292. The premium is deducted from most Social Security checks, so it takes a bite out of the 3.6 percent cost-of-living increase arriving in January; this year the premium increase is smaller than the raise, which has not happened since 2023. People with higher incomes pay an income-related surcharge on top of the standard amount. Beneficiaries will see their exact 2027 deduction in the Social Security notice mailed in December.

Why it matters: For a parent on a fixed income, the net January check is the number to budget care around; the December notice shows it.
