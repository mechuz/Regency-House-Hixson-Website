A Senior Housing News review of the second half of 2026 describes occupancy gains that differ from market to market, while development of new communities remains slow. The report says assisted living absorbed new units equal to about 3 percent of existing inventory, and that building pipelines for independent living and assisted living have shrunk by roughly 60 percent over five years. Deal activity is also part of the picture.

Why it matters: With few new communities being built, families may find fewer openings in popular areas, so starting the search early can help.
