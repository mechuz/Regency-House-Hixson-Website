The City of Chattanooga has opened its 2026 property tax freeze application for homeowners who are 65 or older and meet the program's income limit. The freeze locks the city portion of the property tax bill at its current amount; it does not apply to Hamilton County taxes, which the county commission is considering separately. The application deadline is April 5, 2027. City staff offer in-person help by appointment at City Hall and at several community centers, including a Hixson location, on dates listed in the city's senior programs appointment calendar. Questions go to the city's tax relief line at (423) 643-7274 or taxrelief@chattanooga.gov.

Why it matters: An older parent still living at home in Chattanooga may qualify for a lower city tax bill, and the application help sessions are a practical errand for a family member to handle this winter.
