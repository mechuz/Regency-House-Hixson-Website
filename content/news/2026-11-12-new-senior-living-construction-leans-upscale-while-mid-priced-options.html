Senior Housing News reports on the latest NIC MAP development data: operators had about 88,000 new senior living units in planning across 214 markets as of mid-2026, with roughly seven in ten of them in large primary markets, many on the coasts. Much of the new activity is aimed at higher-end communities, while developers say the math for mid-priced projects is still hard to make work because of construction costs, interest rates and tighter lending. Not every planned project will break ground, and the article notes that the thin pipeline is largely set through 2027.

Why it matters: With few new communities opening in most mid-sized markets, families who find a good fit are better off reserving early than waiting for new options.
