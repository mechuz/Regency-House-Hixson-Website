AARP's 2026 Holiday Shopping and Scams Report finds that most U.S. adults, 84 percent, have run into at least one common kind of fraud. About 47 percent got a fake shipping alert and 44 percent received a text about a fake toll payment in the past year. AARP lists the holiday season as a time to be extra careful with unexpected fees, gift card requests and messages that push you to click a link.

Why it matters: Holiday visits are a good moment to talk with an older parent about scam warning signs and who to call before sending money.
