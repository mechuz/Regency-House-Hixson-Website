Hamilton County commissioners have twice postponed a decision on adopting Tennessee's optional property tax freeze, which would stop county property tax bills from rising for homeowners 65 and older whose income falls under a state-set limit. The item is now scheduled for the commission's early December meeting, which county staff described as the last chance to have the program in place for the 2027 budget. Commissioners asked for figures on how many homeowners would qualify and what the freeze would cost the county. Chattanooga and several other Tennessee counties already offer a version of the freeze.

Why it matters: For a Hixson family deciding whether a parent keeps the house or moves, the December vote changes the math on holding the home; watch the commission agenda.
