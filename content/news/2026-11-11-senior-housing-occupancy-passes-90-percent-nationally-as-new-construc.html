NIC's third-quarter 2026 data shows senior housing occupancy across the 31 largest U.S. markets above 90 percent for the first time since 2007, the 21st straight quarter of gains. Assisted living reached 89.1 percent, up from 88.3 percent the quarter before. The reason is supply: new construction has slowed sharply, so demand from a growing 80-plus population is filling existing communities. NIC expects assisted living to pass 90 percent in the first half of 2027.

Why it matters: Waiting lists are becoming common again; families who are a few months away from needing care should start touring now rather than later.
