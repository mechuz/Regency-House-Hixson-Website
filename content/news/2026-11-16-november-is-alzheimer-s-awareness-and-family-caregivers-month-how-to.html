The Alzheimer's Association marks November as both National Alzheimer's Disease Awareness Month and National Family Caregivers Month. Its page for the month focuses on the people doing the caring and lists concrete ways relatives and friends can lighten the load during the holidays: take over cooking, cleaning or gift shopping, offer to host the family gathering, and set a standing time each week to sit with the person who has dementia so the main caregiver can rest. The association also points caregivers to its 24-hour helpline and local support groups, including those in the Chattanooga area.

Why it matters: If someone in your family is the primary caregiver, the most useful holiday gift is scheduled, dependable time off.
