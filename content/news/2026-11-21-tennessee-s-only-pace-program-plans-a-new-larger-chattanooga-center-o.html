Ascension Living has announced that its Alexian PACE program, the only PACE program in Tennessee, will move into a new 44,000-square-foot Chattanooga center with day centers, a therapy gym, and a medical clinic and pharmacy, expected to open in fall 2027. The program serves Hamilton County and parts of neighboring counties.

Why it matters: PACE is one of the options families in Hamilton County can look into when comparing ways to support an older adult. Eligibility rules are set by the program, so check directly with them.
