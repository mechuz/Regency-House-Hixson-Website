Georgia's first Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly center opened in Savannah on November 10 and is now enrolling, WTOC reports. PACE is a Medicare and Medicaid program for people 55 and older who qualify for a nursing home level of care but can live safely at home or in the community with support. A PACE team coordinates medical care, therapy, medications, transportation and day services under one plan, and the program pays for that care. Georgia passed its PACE law in 2024 and awarded start-up grants to three organizations; this is the first site to open. Tennessee already has PACE programs, and Regency House accepts PACE participants.

Why it matters: PACE can cover the care side for a frail parent who qualifies, which changes the math for families comparing home, assisted living and nursing home options.
