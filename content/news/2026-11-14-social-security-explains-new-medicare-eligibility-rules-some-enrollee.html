The Social Security Administration issued a notice on November 10 describing how a 2025 federal law changes who can be enrolled in Medicare. Eligibility is limited to U.S. citizens and nationals, lawful permanent residents, Cuban and Haitian entrants, and certain Compact of Free Association migrants. People already enrolled whose status cannot be confirmed in federal records will receive a letter and have until February 1, 2027, to document their eligibility or lose Medicare coverage. The agency says Social Security retirement, disability and survivor benefit rules are not affected by the change.

Why it matters: If a parent receives a letter about Medicare eligibility, respond before the deadline and ask a benefits counselor for help; a coverage gap would hit at the worst time.
