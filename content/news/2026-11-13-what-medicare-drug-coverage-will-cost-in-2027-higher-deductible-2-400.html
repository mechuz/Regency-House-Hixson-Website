KFF's analysis of the 2027 Medicare Part D rules lays out what changes on January 1. The standard drug deductible rises to $700 from $615, and the yearly cap on what a person pays out of pocket for covered prescriptions rises to $2,400 from $2,100; once the cap is reached, covered drugs cost nothing for the rest of the year. KFF expects more plans to charge a deductible in 2027 than this year, which makes comparing plans during the open enrollment period that ends December 7 more worthwhile than usual, especially for people on several brand-name medications.

Why it matters: Drug costs are often the line item that surprises families budgeting for a parent's care; the 2027 cap is a known ceiling you can plan around.
