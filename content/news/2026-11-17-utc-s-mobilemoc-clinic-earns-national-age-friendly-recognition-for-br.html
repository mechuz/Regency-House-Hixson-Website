The University of Tennessee at Chattanooga says its MobileMOC program has been recognized as a participant in the national Age-Friendly Health Systems movement, and its care model has been published in NEJM Catalyst. MobileMOC is a mobile primary care and wellness clinic run by UTC's nursing program that visits senior centers and community sites across the ten-county district served by the Southeast Tennessee Area Agency on Aging and Disability, which includes Hamilton County. It serves adults 62 and older and their caregivers regardless of insurance, funded by a Tennessee Department of Health grant, and gives UTC students hands-on training with older patients.

Why it matters: A free, mobile option for check-ups and wellness screenings that older adults in Hixson and the surrounding counties can reach at a local senior center.
