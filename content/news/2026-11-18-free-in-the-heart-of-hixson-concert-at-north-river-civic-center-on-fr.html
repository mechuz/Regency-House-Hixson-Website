The City of Chattanooga's Department of Community Development is inviting residents to a free concert featuring Chattanooga Metro Musicians at the North River Civic Center in Hixson on Friday, November 20, at 7 p.m. The civic center's In the Heart of Hixson series brings local musicians and ensembles to the neighborhood on select Friday evenings through the winter, with hour-long, family-friendly performances and no ticket required. The center is on Executive Drive off Hixson Pike, a short drive from most of the neighborhood.

Why it matters: A free, close-to-home evening out is an easy way for families to include an older relative who no longer drives after dark.
