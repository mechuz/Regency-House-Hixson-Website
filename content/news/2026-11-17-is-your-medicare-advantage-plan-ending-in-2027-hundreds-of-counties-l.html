NPR reports that many Medicare Advantage plans will not be offered in 2027, and that in 193 counties nationwide no Advantage plan is available at all, up from about 120 counties a year earlier according to a KFF analysis. Insurers can also cap enrollment on plans they keep, so waiting can mean a preferred plan is already full. Anyone whose plan is closing should have received a letter in early October. Open enrollment runs through December 7, and Medicare's online Plan Finder lets families compare what is still offered in their county. People whose plan is discontinued get an extra window into early 2027 to choose a new one.

Why it matters: If an older parent is on a Medicare Advantage plan, check the October letter and the Plan Finder before December 7 so coverage does not lapse on January 1.
