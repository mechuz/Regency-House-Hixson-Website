A study in the Journal of the American Geriatrics Society, using nationally representative data on older adults, found that hearing loss was associated with more falls and more fear of falling. About 40 percent of those with functional hearing loss reported a fall in the past year.

Why it matters: If a parent seems to be missing conversation or pulling back from social settings this holiday season, a hearing check is worth raising with their doctor, along with a look at fall risks around the home.
