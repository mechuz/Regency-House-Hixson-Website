McKnight's Senior Living reports on a new survey of direct care employers showing that the share of unfilled healthcare positions fell to about 14 percent between 2024 and 2026, helped by recruiting and retention programs adopted after the pandemic. Assisted living was the exception on volume: openings there rose about 6 percent over the same period, and entry-level roles such as certified nursing assistants made up roughly 44 percent of the vacancies. The piece pairs the survey with the recently released Activated Insights workforce index, which found assisted living turnover falling to about 72 percent after peaking above 84 percent in late 2024.

Why it matters: Staffing is the question behind every tour; ask how long the caregivers you meet have been there and how open shifts are covered.
