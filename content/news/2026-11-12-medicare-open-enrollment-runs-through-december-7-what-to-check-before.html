Kiplinger's guide to the 2027 Medicare open enrollment period walks through the window that closes December 7, 2026. During these weeks a beneficiary can switch Medicare Advantage plans, change a Part D drug plan or return to Original Medicare, with any change taking effect January 1. The guide explains the notices arriving in the mail this month, including the plan's Annual Notice of Change and the Social Security letter that shows next year's premium deductions, and reminds readers that the final 2027 Part B premium is announced by the federal government in November.

Why it matters: An adult child helping a parent in or moving to assisted living has until December 7 to make sure the parent's drug and doctor coverage still fits for 2027.
