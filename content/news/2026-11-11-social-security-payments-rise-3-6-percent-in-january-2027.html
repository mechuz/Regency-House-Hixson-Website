The Social Security Administration announced a 3.6 percent cost-of-living adjustment for 2027. Nearly 72 million people will see the increase in their January payments, and Supplemental Security Income recipients see it starting December 31, 2026. The agency says the average retired worker's benefit rises by roughly $75 a month. People who have not switched to paperless notices will get a letter in December showing their new amount. Part of the increase is usually absorbed by the Medicare Part B premium, which is deducted from most checks.

Why it matters: If a parent's monthly budget for care is tight, the new January figure is the number to plan around; check the December notice for the exact amount.
