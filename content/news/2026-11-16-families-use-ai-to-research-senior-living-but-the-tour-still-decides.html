U.S. News & World Report surveyed about 500 adults who had recently moved a relative into senior living or were actively searching. Most now use AI tools and online search to build a shortlist of communities, but the survey found that the in-person visit and the people a family meets on tour day are what settle the final choice. Respondents still value a clear written explanation of services, a chance to talk with current residents and staff, and a community that answers questions directly. Half of those surveyed had completed a move in 2025 or 2026 and half were in the middle of a search.

Why it matters: Online research narrows the list, but a family should still plan to visit, ask questions in person and see a meal or activity before deciding.
