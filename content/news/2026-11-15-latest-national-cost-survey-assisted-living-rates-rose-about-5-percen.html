CareScout's annual Cost of Care Survey, the long-running national benchmark formerly published by Genworth, collected more than 25,000 provider rates between July and November 2025. Its 2026 release puts the national median for an assisted living community at about $6,200 a month, roughly 5 percent higher than the year before. Trade coverage noted that long-term care cost growth slowed in 2025 after several years of steep increases. The survey also shows large differences between states and between care settings, and CareScout cautions that actual costs depend on a person's care needs, local availability and the services included.

Why it matters: A national median is only a starting point; families should ask any community for a written breakdown of what its monthly rate includes before comparing options.
