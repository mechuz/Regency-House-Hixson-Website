Senior Housing News reports a growing trend among senior living operators: cognitive wellness and brain-health offerings, once concentrated in memory care settings, are being extended to residents in independent living and assisted living. The piece describes the shift as part of a broader move toward activities and programming aimed at keeping older adults mentally engaged earlier, not only after a diagnosis.

Why it matters: When touring, families can ask any community what daily activities and programs are offered for mental and social engagement, and how those are chosen and led.
