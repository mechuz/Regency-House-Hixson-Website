About Regency House

A licensed assisted living community on Hamill Road in Hixson, Tennessee.

Our Community

Personal Care in a Setting That Feels Like Home

Regency House is a licensed Assisted Care Living Facility in Hixson, Tennessee, with forty-eight apartments and a license for up to fifty-four residents. We have served this community for many years, and most of the families who choose us live within a short drive.

Our focus is deliberate: assisted living and respite care, provided consistently and with respect for each resident’s dignity and independence. We are not a skilled nursing facility and do not operate a secure memory care unit, and we say so clearly in our first conversation so that families can make an informed decision.

Our Commitments

How We Serve Residents and Families

  • Honesty about cost and fit. We discuss rates, availability and whether we are the right community for your family openly, by telephone, before you visit.
  • Dignity and independence. We assist with what has become difficult and respect each resident’s ability to do the rest. Care plans are reviewed regularly as needs change.
  • Compliance and accountability. We operate under Tennessee Department of Health licensure and welcome families to review our public inspection record.
  • Direct, personal service. When you call, you reach our team in Hixson. Your enquiry is handled here and is never passed to a referral service.

Licensing

Licensed by the State of Tennessee

Regency House Assisted Living is licensed by the Tennessee Department of Health, Board for Licensing Health Care Facilities, as an Assisted Care Living Facility — license #0537-00000150. State inspection records for every licensed facility in Tennessee are public, and we encourage families to review ours.
