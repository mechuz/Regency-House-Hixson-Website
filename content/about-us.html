About Regency House

A licensed assisted living residence on Hamill Road in Hixson, Tennessee — boutique by design, attentive by conviction.

Who We Are

A Residence, Not a Campus

Regency House is a licensed Assisted Care Living Facility in Hixson, Tennessee — forty-eight apartments, licensed for up to fifty-four residents. We have been part of this neighborhood for many years, and most of the families who entrust their loved ones to us live within a short drive.

What we offer is deliberately focused: assisted living and respite residence, delivered with care and consistency, in a house small enough that no one is ever reduced to a room number. We are not a skilled nursing facility, and we do not operate a secure memory care wing — and we will say so candidly in our first conversation, rather than allow a family to discover it after a move.

Our Convictions

How We Conduct Ourselves

  • Candour, early. Rates, availability and whether we are the right fit — discussed openly by telephone, before you make the drive to Hixson.
  • Independence, preserved. Assistance with what has become unsafe or wearying, and nothing beyond it. A care plan is revisited as needs evolve, never set once and forgotten.
  • Limits, acknowledged. Should a resident ever require more than an assisted living licence permits us to provide, we say so plainly and help the family plan what comes next.
  • No intermediaries. When you call, you reach the community in Hixson. Your enquiry is never sold or passed along to a dozen facilities.

Licensing

Licensed by the State of Tennessee

Regency House Assisted Living is licensed by the Tennessee Department of Health, Board for Licensing Health Care Facilities, as an Assisted Care Living Facility — license #0537-00000150. State inspection records for every licensed facility in Tennessee are a matter of public record, and we encourage families to consult ours.
