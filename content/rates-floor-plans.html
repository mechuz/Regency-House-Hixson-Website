Our Pricing

How the Monthly Rate Is Determined

Regency House is committed to quality care at a rate families can afford. There are no care levels or tiered pricing. One monthly rate, set by the apartment you choose, covers the apartment and all of the care and services we provide.

The apartment you choose

A studio or a one-bedroom, each with its own kitchenette and private bathroom. Larger apartments are priced accordingly. Availability changes from week to week, and we will tell you exactly what is open when you call.

Everything else is included

Personal care, medication management, three meals a day, housekeeping, laundry, activities and transportation are all part of the rate. A resident who needs more help is not charged more than one who needs less.

Transparent Pricing

Your Rate, Quoted in Writing

Because the rate depends on the apartment and on current availability, we do not publish a single figure online. Call us and we will give you a clear monthly estimate in about ten minutes, including what long-term care insurance or VA benefits may offset. Every one-time cost is set out in writing before you sign. You will know the full cost before you make the drive to Hixson.

Included

What the Monthly Rate Includes

One rate covers the apartment and the care. None of the following is billed separately:

  • Private apartment with kitchenette
  • Three meals daily, with snacks
  • Personal care assistance
  • Medication management by our nursing team
  • Staff on site 24 hours a day and the emergency call system
  • Weekly housekeeping and personal laundry
  • The activity calendar and scheduled transportation

Paying for Care

Payment Options

  • Private pay — income, savings, or the proceeds of a home sale
  • Long-term care insurance — we will help you review the policy and file the claim
  • VA benefits — Aid and Attendance can offset a meaningful part of the monthly cost for eligible veterans and surviving spouses

Coverage depends on the specific policy or eligibility. Please call, and we will go through what applies and what documentation is required.

Please note: Medicare does not pay for assisted living room and board at any community. It may cover specific medical services a resident receives. For TennCare and the CHOICES program, please ask what we are currently able to accept, as the rules change.

Common Questions

Questions About Cost

Is there a community fee or move-in fee?

Please ask us directly when you call. We will set out every one-time cost in writing before anything is signed, so there are no surprises at move-in.

Does the monthly rate change if care needs increase?

No. Regency House does not use care levels or tiered pricing. The rate is set by the apartment, and the care and services included do not change the price as needs change.

Do you accept Medicare or TennCare?

Medicare does not pay for assisted living room and board at any community, although it may cover specific medical services a resident receives. For TennCare and the CHOICES program, please call and ask what we are currently able to accept, as participation and eligibility rules change.

Can we tour before discussing cost?

Yes, and the reverse is equally welcome. Many families prefer to know the rate first so they can tour with confidence. Either order works for us.
