How the Rate Is Composed

Two Considerations, and Nothing Else

1. The apartment you choose

A studio or a one-bedroom, each with its own kitchenette. The larger apartments are priced accordingly. Availability changes from week to week, and we will tell you precisely what is open when you call.

2. The measure of daily care

A nursing assessment establishes how much hands-on assistance is required. A resident who needs a reminder and a steady arm is not charged as one who needs full assistance morning and evening.

Included

What the Monthly Rate Comprises

One rate encompasses the residence and the care. None of the following is billed separately:

  • Private apartment with kitchenette
  • Three meals daily, with refreshments
  • Personal care assistance
  • Medication administration by licensed nurses
  • A care team present day and night, and the emergency call system
  • Weekly housekeeping and personal laundry
  • The activity calendar and scheduled transportation

Meeting the Cost

How Families Provide for Care

  • Private resources — income, savings, or the proceeds of a home
  • Long-term care insurance — we are glad to help you read the policy and file the claim
  • VA benefits — Aid and Attendance can meaningfully offset the monthly cost for eligible veterans and surviving spouses

Coverage depends on the particular policy or eligibility. Please call, and we will go through what applies and what documentation is required.

Questions of Cost

What Families Ask About the Rate

Is there a community fee or move-in fee?

Please ask us directly when you call, and we will set out every one-time cost in writing before anything is signed. No family should encounter a fee at signing — from us or from anyone.

Does the monthly rate rise if care needs increase?

Yes — should the level of daily care change, the rate is adjusted accordingly. We reassess regularly and discuss any change with you before it takes effect, so that it remains a conversation rather than a surprise on an invoice.

Do you accept Medicare or TennCare?

Medicare does not pay for assisted living room and board anywhere — a common and costly misunderstanding. It may cover specific medical services a resident receives. For TennCare and the CHOICES programme, please call and ask what we are currently able to accept, as participation and eligibility rules change.

May we tour before discussing cost?

Of course — and the reverse is equally welcome. Many families prefer to know the figure first, so as not to tour a residence beyond their means. Either order suits us well.
