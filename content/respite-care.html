Respite Care

You Are Allowed to Need a Break

Most people caring for a parent at home do it for years without a single week off. It is not sustainable, and running yourself into the ground does not help the person you are caring for.

A respite stay is a short, planned stay at Regency House while you travel, recover, work, or simply sleep. Respite residents get the same care as everyone else: a private apartment, three meals a day, medications administered by licensed nurses, housekeeping, activities and a 24-hour care team.

When Families Use It

Reasons for a Short Stay

  • A caregiver needs time — a holiday, a work trip, surgery, or plain burnout
  • Recovery after a hospital stay — when going home alone is not safe yet
  • A trial run — a real stay before committing to a permanent move
  • A gap in home care — when an aide leaves or a schedule falls apart
  • Weather or winter — when living alone becomes a risk for a few months

The Trial Run

It Turns an Argument Into an Answer

Your parent gets a say

Instead of arguing about a hypothetical, they experience the place. Many residents who resisted the idea entirely decide for themselves after a week.

You see the real thing

A tour shows you a building. A stay shows you how a call gets answered at nine at night, and whether staff know your parent’s name by day three.

Nothing is permanent

If it is not right, they go home and you have lost nothing but a few weeks’ cost. If it is right, the hardest decision is already made.

Practical Questions

What to Expect

How short can a respite stay be?

Stays are typically a few days to several weeks, depending on availability and on your parent’s care needs. Call (423) 870-0050 with your dates and we will tell you what is possible.

How is a respite stay priced?

Short-term stays are generally charged at a daily rate covering the apartment, meals, personal care and medication administration. Because the rate depends on the apartment and the level of care needed, we quote it directly — call and we will give you the number for your dates.

What should we bring?

Comfortable clothing for the length of the stay, toiletries, any mobility equipment, and a complete current medication list with the prescribing physicians. We will go through the full list with you before arrival so nothing is a surprise on the first day.

Is an assessment still required for a short stay?

Yes. Even for a few days we complete a short nursing assessment of health history, medications and mobility. It is how we keep every resident safe, and it is required under our assisted living licence.

Can a respite stay become permanent?

Often, yes — that is one reason families choose respite first. If your parent settles in and an apartment is available, we can usually convert a short-term stay into a permanent residency without another move.
