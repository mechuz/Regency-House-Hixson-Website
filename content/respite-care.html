Respite Residence

A Considered Interval

Most people caring for a parent at home do so for years without a single week’s rest. It is not sustainable, and exhausting oneself serves no one — least of all the person being cared for.

A respite stay is a short, planned residence at Regency House while you travel, recover, work, or simply rest. Respite guests receive precisely the care our permanent residents do: a private apartment, three meals a day, medications administered by licensed nurses, housekeeping, the activity calendar, and a care team present through the night.

When Families Choose It

Occasions for a Short Stay

  • A caregiver requires time — travel, an engagement, surgery, or simply rest long overdue
  • Convalescence after a hospital stay — when home alone is not yet safe
  • A graceful introduction — a genuine stay before any longer commitment
  • An interval in home care — when an aide departs or arrangements falter
  • The winter months — when living alone becomes a concern for a season

Practical Matters

What to Expect

How brief may a respite stay be?

Stays are typically a few days to several weeks, according to availability and your parent’s care needs. Telephone (423) 870-0050 with your dates and we will tell you what can be arranged.

How is a respite stay priced?

Short-term residence is generally charged at a daily rate covering the apartment, meals, personal care and medication administration. As the rate depends on the apartment and the level of care required, we quote it directly — please call and we will provide the figure for your dates.

What should we bring?

Comfortable clothing for the length of the stay, toiletries, any mobility equipment, and a complete current list of medications with the prescribing physicians. We review the full list with you before arrival, so that nothing is a surprise on the first day.

Is an assessment required for a short stay?

Yes. Even for a few days we complete a brief nursing assessment of health history, medications and mobility. It is how we keep every resident safe, and it is required under our assisted living licence.

May a respite stay become permanent?

Often, yes — it is one reason families choose respite first. Should your parent settle in and an apartment be available, we can usually convert a short stay into a permanent residence without a second move.
